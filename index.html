    About

    This is OpenAI's GPT-2 small (124M parameters, 2019), compressed to 8-bit and running in plain TypeScript in a Web Worker. Weights are served from this site and cached by your browser. Attention patterns are read straight out of the forward pass, not approximated.

    The model is small and old: its guesses are often wrong, odd or biased. That is part of what makes it worth looking inside.
